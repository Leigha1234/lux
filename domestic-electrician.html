Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about domestic electrical services.

How do I know if my home's electrics need upgrading?

Signs that your electrical installation may need attention include frequently tripping circuits, damaged sockets or switches, flickering lights, outdated fuse boxes and a lack of sockets for modern appliances. If your property has an older electrical installation, an Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR) can help assess its condition and identify any defects or potential safety concerns.

Do I need to upgrade my consumer unit when having new electrical work carried out?

Not always. Whether your consumer unit needs upgrading depends on the condition of your existing installation, the type of electrical work being undertaken and the protection available. We can assess your current installation and advise whether an upgrade is recommended to improve safety and accommodate your electrical requirements.

How much does it cost to hire a domestic electrician?

The cost of hiring an electrician depends on the type and complexity of the work required, the materials involved and the time needed to complete the job.
