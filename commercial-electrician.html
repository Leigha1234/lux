Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about commercial electrical services.

Do you carry out electrical work in offices, shops and other commercial premises?

Yes, we undertake electrical work across a variety of commercial environments, including offices, retail units, workshops and other business premises. Whether you require additional power supplies, lighting upgrades, new circuits or a larger electrical installation, we provide professional solutions tailored to your property's needs.

Do commercial properties require regular electrical inspections and testing?

Regular electrical inspections and testing help identify deterioration, damage and potential electrical hazards before they become more serious problems. The recommended inspection frequency depends on the type of premises, its use and the condition of the installation. Lux Electrical Engineering Ltd provides EICRs to assess the condition of electrical installations and identify any remedial work required.

Can you carry out electrical work while minimising disruption to my business?

We understand the importance of keeping your business operating efficiently. Where practical, electrical work can be planned around your operating hours and site requirements to help minimise disruption. We discuss the scope of work, access arrangements and any necessary power interruptions in advance to help ensure the project is completed safely and efficiently.
