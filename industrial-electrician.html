Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about industrial electrical services.

Do you provide industrial electrical maintenance and fault finding?

Yes, we can assist with industrial electrical faults, testing and maintenance requirements. Identifying electrical issues promptly can help reduce disruption, improve reliability and maintain safe working conditions. We investigate electrical problems and recommend appropriate remedial work based on our findings and the requirements of your installation.

Can you install electrical containment systems in factories and warehouses?

Lux Electrical Engineering Ltd provides electrical containment installations for industrial and commercial environments. This includes galvanised trunking, conduit, cable tray and associated cable management systems. Properly planned containment helps protect cables, maintain an organised installation and support future access for inspection and maintenance.

Do you carry out electrical inspection and testing in industrial facilities?

Yes, we provide electrical inspection and testing services to assess the condition and safety of industrial electrical installations. This can include Electrical Installation Condition Reports (EICRs), circuit testing and the inspection of distribution boards and protective devices. We identify defects and provide clear reporting to help businesses plan remedial work and maintain their electrical systems in a safe condition.
