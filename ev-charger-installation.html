Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about ev charger installation.

What types of EV chargers can you install?

Lux Electrical Engineering Ltd provides electric vehicle charger installations for domestic and commercial properties. Whether you need a home charging point for overnight charging or a charging solution for your business, we can help you choose a suitable charger based on your vehicle, electrical installation and charging requirements.

Do I need to upgrade my consumer unit to install an EV charger?

Not necessarily. Whether your consumer unit needs upgrading depends on the condition of your existing electrical installation, available capacity, earthing arrangements and the requirements of the charger. We assess the existing installation and advise on any necessary upgrades or additional protective measures before installation.

Can you install an EV charger if my property has limited electrical capacity?

Yes, an EV charger may still be possible where electrical capacity is limited. Depending on the existing supply and the charger's requirements, load management equipment, such as a CT clamp, can help regulate charging demand and reduce the risk of overloading the electrical supply. We assess your installation and advise on a suitable solution, including any DNO notification or approval requirements.
